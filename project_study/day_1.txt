## LTAFDB rhythm labels (aux_note)

These appear in the aux_note column, always with symbol "+"
("+" = rhythm changes here, aux_note = what it changes to).
A label means: "from here on, the rhythm is this — until the next label."

| aux_note | Rhythm                            | In plain words                                   |
|----------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| (N       | Normal sinus rhythm               | Normal heartbeat                                 |
| (AFIB    | Atrial fibrillation               | AF — the one we care about                       |
| (SBR     | Sinus bradycardia                 | Normal rhythm, but slow                          |
| (SVTA    | Supraventricular tachyarrhythmia  | Fast rhythm starting above the lower chambers    |
| (B       | Ventricular bigeminy              | Every other beat is an early V beat              |
| (T       | Ventricular trigeminy             | Every third beat is an early V beat              |
| (IVR     | Idioventricular rhythm            | Lower chambers set a slow pace themselves        |
| (VT      | Ventricular tachycardia           | Fast rhythm from the lower chambers (serious)    |
